# Spatial Rendering Evaluation

This context names the opt-in evidence workflow used to evaluate frontal binaural
externalization without changing the frozen production defaults.

## Language

**Natural-Level Render**:
A condition rendered with its own configured production gain staging intact.
_Avoid_: Unmatched render, raw render

**Level-Matched Evaluation Render**:
An evaluation-only copy matched to condition A by BS.1770 integrated loudness;
its gain is never written back into the renderer.
_Avoid_: Normalized production render, RMS match

**Screening Excerpt**:
A fixed 20-second section used to reject weak or harmful single-factor conditions
before expensive full-track listening.
_Avoid_: Dry vocal, stem

**Maturity Full-Track**:
One of the three complete stereo songs used only after a condition passes excerpt
screening; no source separation or stem extraction is involved.
_Avoid_: Vocal stem, duet stem

**Frontal Probe Case**:
One deterministic angle-distance combination rendered from a synthetic engineering
probe to expose objective changes without making a perceptual claim.
_Avoid_: Listening reference, proof of externalization

**FEX-1 Maturity**:
Repeatable research evidence across the pinned stereo corpus with no important
clarity, stability, timbre, double-image, or mono regression; it is not promotion.
_Avoid_: Production ready, default promotion
